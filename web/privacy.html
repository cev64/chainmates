Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026 · Applies to the Chainmates website

The short version

  • There are no accounts. You never give us your name, email or anything else.
  • We collect nothing. There are no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
  • Your games, streaks and settings are saved only in your own browser (its local storage). They never leave your device.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts, so those two services see the ordinary technical details of your visit, such as your IP address.
  • Sharing a result is up to you, and the share text contains no player names or personal information.

Who we are

Chainmates (“we”, “us”) is a free, independent daily puzzle made by an individual developer. It is an unofficial fan game and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the NFL, the NFLPA or any team.

What we collect

Nothing. Chainmates has no accounts, no sign-in, no server of its own and no database. We do not receive, store or sell any information about you.

On your device

To remember your place, the site saves a few small items in your browser’s local storage. They stay on your device and are never sent to us:

  • your progress on each puzzle you play (the players you added, downs used and hints taken), kept separately for daily and practice games;
  • your statistics: games played, wins, streaks and how your results compare with par;
  • your theme choice (light, dark or automatic) and whether you have seen the How to play screen.

These items use names that start with chainmates.. They are not cookies and they are not shared with anyone.

Hosting and fonts

The site is served by GitHub Pages, and its typefaces (Inter and Barlow Condensed) are loaded from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches these files, GitHub and Google receive the technical details any website request carries, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request. They handle that information under their own privacy policies: see GitHub’s Privacy Statement and Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not receive those logs.

The player data file is part of the site itself, so loading a puzzle does not contact any other service.

Analytics, ads and tracking

None. Chainmates does not use analytics, advertising, cookies, fingerprinting or any other tracking. If that ever changes, we will update this policy first.

Sharing results

When you press Share or Copy, the site puts a short summary of your result on your clipboard or hands it to your device’s share menu. It contains the puzzle number, coloured squares, your score and a link, and no player names or personal details. Where it goes after that is up to you.

Children

Chainmates collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can delete everything Chainmates has saved at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings. This resets your games and statistics on that device. Private or incognito windows forget it when they close.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at github.com/cev64/handoff/issues or see the Support page.
